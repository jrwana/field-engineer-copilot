# AN-101 — Retimer Placement and Channel Reach (Application Note)

> Fictional company and products, created for a portfolio project.

## Channel loss budgets
The end-to-end insertion loss budget at Nyquist is about 36 dB for PCIe 5.0 (32 GT/s, 16 GHz) and about 32 dB for PCIe 6.0 (64 GT/s PAM4, 16 GHz). PCIe 6.0 has a tighter budget because PAM4 signaling has less voltage margin per symbol.

## When to add a retimer
Estimate the total channel loss, including package, board traces, connectors and cables. Keep at least 4 dB of margin. If the estimated loss is greater than the budget minus 4 dB, add a retimer. For PCIe 6.0 this means a retimer is recommended above 28 dB; for PCIe 5.0, above 32 dB.

## Retimers in series
The PCIe specification allows at most two retimers on one link. Halo R-Series parts follow this limit: you can cascade up to two retimers between a root complex and an endpoint, never three.

## Reference clocking
Halo R-Series retimers support common clock (CC), separate reference clock with no spread spectrum (SRNS), and separate reference clock with spread spectrum (SRIS). On Halo R5 silicon revision A1, SRIS has a known issue; see ER-R5-003.
