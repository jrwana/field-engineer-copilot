# PB-ETHERNET — Halo E8 800G Ethernet Retimer (Product Brief)

> Fictional company and products, created for a portfolio project.

## Overview
The HX-E800 is an 800GbE retimer with 8 lanes of 112 Gb/s PAM4 (112G per lane). It is used in active electrical cables (AECs) and on switch and NIC line cards to extend reach.

## Reach and latency
In an AEC, the HX-E800 supports copper cable lengths up to 3 m at 800GbE. Typical added latency is 4 ns.

## Not a PCIe device
The HX-E800 is an Ethernet device. It does not support PCIe or CXL.

## Power
Typical power is 11 W.
