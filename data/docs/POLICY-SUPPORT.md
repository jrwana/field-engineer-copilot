# POLICY-SUPPORT — What Field Engineers May Answer

> Fictional company and products, created for a portfolio project.

## Allowed
Information in public product briefs, application notes, errata, firmware release notes and the public product catalog.

## Must escalate
- Pricing, quotes, discounts and lead times: escalate to Sales Operations.
- Roadmap, future or unannounced products: escalate to Product Management.
- NDA-only material, such as validated platform lists or die-level data: escalate to the customer's Account Manager.
- Reliability data such as MTBF or FIT rates: escalate to the Quality team.
- Part numbers that are not in the catalog: do not guess specs. Ask the customer to confirm the part number and escalate if needed.

## Unclear requests
If a question cannot be answered without more information (for example, "our link is unstable"), ask the customer for specifics: part number, firmware version, link speed and width, clocking mode, and symptoms.
