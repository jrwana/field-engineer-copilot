# AN-204 — Lane Margining and Link Insight Diagnostics (Application Note)

> Fictional company and products, created for a portfolio project.

## What lane margining does
Receiver lane margining measures how much timing and voltage margin each lane has while the link is running, without taking the link down. It is the fastest way to find a marginal lane on a new board.

## Supported parts
Lane margining is supported on Halo R5 (HX-R5008, HX-R5016) and Halo R6 (HX-R6008, HX-R6016). It is not supported on Halo R4 (HX-R4016).

## Firmware requirement
Lane margining in Halo Link Insight requires retimer firmware 2.4.0 or later. On older firmware, such as 2.3.x, the margining panel does not appear. Update the firmware first.

## Access methods
Link Insight connects to the retimer over SMBus/I2C or in-band over PCIe. It reports per-lane eye width and height, link training history and error counters. The `hx status` command also shows the silicon revision.
