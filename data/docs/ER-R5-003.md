# ER-R5-003 — Halo R5 Link Trains to 16 GT/s with SRIS Clocking (Erratum)

> Fictional company and products, created for a portfolio project.

## Affected parts
HX-R5008 and HX-R5016, silicon revision A1.

## Symptom
When the system uses SRIS clocking (separate reference clock with spread spectrum), the link may train to 16 GT/s (PCIe 4.0 rate) instead of 32 GT/s with some endpoints.

## Workaround
Update the retimer firmware to version 2.4.1 or later. The firmware adjusts the clock-recovery settings and restores 32 GT/s operation.

## Fix
Fixed in silicon revision B0, shipping since Q2 2026. Revision B0 parts have "B0" in the top marking. You can also read the revision with the `hx status` command in Link Insight.
